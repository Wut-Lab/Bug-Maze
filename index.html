🐞 Ladybug Maze: Blueprint

A toddler game where a ladybug (and later other insects) hides through a maze. Art style follows Dino-Match.

1. Concept & Core Loop
  • Toddler drags or taps to guide the ladybug through a simple maze to a hiding spot (a leaf, flower, or log).
  • Arrive at the spot → ladybug hides → happy animation + sound → next maze.
  • No fail state, no timer. Wrong turns just bump gently.
2. Art Style (from Dino-Match)
  • Reuse Dino-Match palette, rounded shapes, thick outlines, and the soft background.
  • Characters: ladybug first; later bee, caterpillar, butterfly, ant.
  • Maze walls look like hedges or grass so it reads as a garden.
3. Toddler UX Rules
  • Big touch targets (at least 60px), paths wide enough for a small finger.
  • No reading needed: icons and voice/sound cues only.
  • Mazes start very short (2–3 turns) and grow slowly.
4. Asset: Ladybug

Guide the ladybug through a new random hedge maze to the leaf. Tap to send it, or drag to lead it. Reaching the leaf hides it and builds a slightly bigger maze (3×3 up to 6×6).

  • Pure SVG: scales cleanly, easy to recolor for other insects.
  • Draft style. Match to Dino-Match outlines and palette once shared.
5. Maze Generator

Random maze each time (depth-first "backtracker": always solvable, one path between any two spots). Size grows with level.

  • Start: top-left. Hiding spot: bottom-right leaf.
  • Walls styled as hedges; wide paths for small fingers.
